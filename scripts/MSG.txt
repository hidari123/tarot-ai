fix: share card summary truncation — show full closing passage

User report: share card text cut off after a few lines.

Two stacked causes:
- page.tsx passed only reading.slice(0, 120) as the summary
- canvas rendered at most 4 lines of it

Fixes:
- extractShareSummary(): pick the closing passage (last paragraph, usually
  the 启示/收束 summary with the highest share value) instead of hard
  truncating the front; falls back to joining the previous paragraph when
  the last is very short; strips 【追问】/【补充】interjections; caps at
  360 chars for the card
- Canvas: 8 lines max (was 4), font size adapts to passage length
  (26/28/32px), line height follows font, and when the full reading is
  longer than what's shown, a "——完整解读见占卜正文——" hint is drawn
- ShareCardData gains fullReadingLength so the canvas knows whether the
  reading continues beyond the shown lines

Layout verified: cards end ~y770, summary 8×~48px from y844 ends ~y1230,
footer at y1530 — no overlap.
